**Kept for the record.** An early version from 2026-10-15, before the one-click `BUILD.bat` (v26). To play, use the newest release.

**Fix stutter after battles / leaving town; fix touch at screen edges**

- Stutter: the game clears VRAM that is unmapped at that moment (ignored
  by the DS); every write printed a warning, ~1800 lines in one frame,
  which takes over half a second on a Windows console. The log now prints
  at most 32 lines per frame and a count of the rest.
- Touch: the firmware's touch calibration put the top and bottom rows
  outside the 12-bit ADC range, so they wrapped around (dragging near an
  edge moved the other way). New calibration keeps every pixel in range
  with exact power-of-two steps; readings are clamped like the hardware
  and a drag that leaves the screen stays on its edge.
- Sampler (test builds) only samples quickly inside its frame window.
