**Kept for the record.** A nightly version from 2026-10-18: the newest work of its day, less tested. To play, use the newest release.

**DQVC and the quest download built in, no extras needed**

DQVC's 52 sales are built in as numbers (no item names or sale titles), and
the quest download is built from the quest and guest numbers it held - the
same file, byte for byte, as Nintendo's of June 2010. BUILD.bat fetches the
one surviving DQVC news message from the fans' archive. Original files in
extras/ are still used if present.
