**Modern menu - walk in any direction with the left stick, top screen in the corner**

Walking: the game already walks in any direction by stylus drag. While the
hero is free to walk (the game's own flag), the left stick now does that
drag; elsewhere it stays a D-pad. To the game and to friends in a session it
is just the stylus. Layout: the field fills a 16:9 window with the top
screen a third of its size in the corner (View, F2). Both off by default;
with them off, replays are unchanged.
