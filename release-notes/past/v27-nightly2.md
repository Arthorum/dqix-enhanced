**Kept for the record.** A nightly version from 2026-10-17: the newest work of its day, less tested. To play, use the newest release.

**Wireless > Play with friends far away (lobby, router port, name), docs**

The window: Host / Join / Start / Leave / Your name in the Wireless menu; the
game restarts into the session (save first). Lobby on the DS screens (system
font via stb_truetype): the address to give friends (copied), who's in,
pings. The host's game asks the router to forward the port (UPnP) and shows
the internet address; otherwise IPv6 or the home address and what to do.
Title bar: players, input delay, news, OUT OF SYNC. Hidden consoles that
stop by themselves are noticed. Waiting naps after 2 ms instead of spinning.
Runtime built with -ftrivial-auto-var-init=zero (same results on every PC).
