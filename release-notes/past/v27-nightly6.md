**DQVC sales on Fridays at 08:00 UTC, new stock every day**

Each year's cycle starts on the Friday nearest 15 July and every sale keeps
its offset from it, as in 2011-12. Days start at 08:00 UTC, when the
original service put out new stock, and each day serves 12 of the sale's
items picked by the date; the game shows six of them. The UTC offset is
carried by sessions and bug reports so they stay reproducible.
