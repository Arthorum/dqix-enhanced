Dragon Quest IX for PC
======================

This turns YOUR OWN Dragon Quest IX ROM (the USA version, dumped from your
cartridge) into a PC version of the game. No game files are included.

1. Put your ROM (.nds) in the "rom" folder.
2. Double-click BUILD.bat.
   - The first time it installs free build tools (MSYS2) and takes 10-20 minutes.
   - It needs Windows 10/11 (64-bit), ~3 GB free space and the internet once.
3. Play: double-click game\dqix.exe (make a desktop shortcut to it if you like).

Controls: arrows = D-pad, X = A, Z = B, S = X, A = Y, Q/W = L/R,
Enter = START, Right Shift = SELECT, mouse = touch screen, Tab = fast forward.
Gamepads work too. Every setting is in the menu bar at the top of the window.

If something goes wrong, press F10 in the game: it saves a bug report in
game\reports\ (with a box to describe what happened). Send that folder.

Please don't share the "game" or "build" folders: they contain the game.
